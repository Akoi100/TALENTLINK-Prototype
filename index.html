4. User Journey & Workflow Models

4.1 Patron Workflow (Logging a Match Offline)

  1. Event Setup: The football coach opens the app on a tablet while at a remote tournament field with zero cellular coverage.
  2. Data Entry: The coach selects the fixture, inputs goals scored, lists assists, and logs disciplinary records for each player.
  3. Local Persistence: The application writes the records directly to the local SQLite database, marking the transaction status as Pending_Sync.
  4. Automatic Synchronization: Days later, when the tablet connects to school Wi-Fi or cellular data, the background sync pushes payload to cloud server, reconciling IDs and updating the global ledger without user intervention.

4.2 Scout Workflow (Talent Discovery)

  1. Filtering: A high school sports scholarship coordinator filters the database for students aged 14 to 16 with a verified rating in long-distance running and an academic conduct score above a set threshold.
  2. Portfolio Review: The scout views the candidate's verified timeline, watches compressed performance video clips, and reviews official certificates issued by school patrons.
  3. Secure Outreach: The scout initiates an encrypted inquiry through the platform, which routes a notification to the school principal and activity patron for approval.

5. Security, Privacy, and Compliance

Data Minimization & Minor Protection

Because users are minors, personally identifiable information (PII) such as home addresses and precise contact details are masked. Communication between scouts and students is strictly mediated through institutional accounts (Patrons/Administrators).

Tamper-Evident Records

Badges and milestone logs incorporate cryptographic signatures (SHA-256 hashes linking student ID, timestamp, and issuing patron ID), preventing unauthorized local database manipulation.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
  • Students/Parents: Read-only access to their own cumulative portfolio.
  • Patrons/Coaches: Write/Edit access for their specific assigned clubs and matches.
  • Administrators: School-wide oversight and verification rights.
  • Scouts: Read access limited to public-facing talent profiles approved for recruitment pools.
